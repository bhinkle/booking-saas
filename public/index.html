A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s clear
things up.

Who is this platform being built for?

Recreational rental operators, starting with jet skis, boats, pontoons, golf carts, and ATVs. The scheduling and inventory model is planned to support more rental industries over time.

Can I use my business branding?

Yes—business branding and configurable booking website themes are part of the planned platform, along with support for business domains.

How will payments work?

Software subscription payments and your customers’ rental payments will be handled separately. Merchant processing will require its own onboarding. Payment options and terms will be confirmed before launch.

Is the software available today?

The platform is in development. This website is an early introduction, and the calendar above is an illustrative concept. Launch timing and confirmed features will be announced when ready.

Will there be a mobile app?

An operator mobile app is a future goal. The initial focus is a reliable web booking experience and business dashboard.
